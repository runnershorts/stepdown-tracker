The Step-Down Playbook

Replace the habit and ritual rather than relying on 6:00 PM willpower. Use these rules week-by-week.

Week 1 (Days 1–7) 85–90% Baseline

Audit & Delay

The 60-Minute Buffer: Don't cut volume aggressively yet—change timing. Start every evening with a cold NA drink in proper glassware first. Once you quench thirst and pass the 45-minute transition peak, urges drop sharply.

Week 2 (Days 8–14) 65–75% Baseline

Protect Weeknights

Sun–Thu Guardrail: Designate 3–4 weeknights to be completely alcohol-free so you remove daily negotiation fatigue. Keep craft NA beers, hop waters, or tonic with bitters and lime front and center in the fridge.

Week 3 (Days 15–21) 45–50% Baseline

Lower ABV & Pace

1-for-1 & ABV Downshift: Alternate every alcoholic drink with a full sparkling water. Swap high-ABV IPAs, heavy reds, or neat pours for session beers (≤4.5% ABV) or wine spritzers to halve ethanol load.

Week 4 (Days 22–30) 25–35% Baseline

Intentional Capping

Pre-Decided Cap & NA First: Decide your exact drink limit before walking into a dinner or event. Order an NA drink first upon arrival so you immediately have a glass in hand, then switch back to NA once your cap is reached.

High-Friction vs. High-Reward Ritual Swaps

  • ✓ Upgrade the glass: Pour NA beer into a chilled pint glass or tonic + bitters into a rocks glass with a large ice cube and citrus peel.
  • ✓ Move the stash: Relocate alcohol out of eye level in the main fridge; put cold NA substitutes right up front.
  • ✓ Track recovery & strength upside: Watch your Garmin Sleep Score, RHR, mental clarity, and Estimated 1RM climb on dry weeks.
