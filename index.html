The 30-Day Peak Health Playbook

Build lasting momentum across sleep, body composition, strength training, and mindful drinking—one week at a time.

Week 1 (Days 1–7) Baseline & Rhythm

Lock In Daily Signals

Measure What Matters: Log morning weight, Garmin sleep score, RHR, and mental clarity every day. Use the 60-minute evening buffer with a cold NA drink first to calm end-of-day transition urges.

Week 2 (Days 8–14) Protect Recovery

Stack Deep Sleep & Training

Sun–Thu Recovery Guardrail: Keep 3–4 weeknights alcohol-free so your RHR drops and Garmin sleep climbs into the 80s–90s. Pair dry nights with morning strength sessions or Zone 2 cardio.

Week 3 (Days 15–21) Strength & Pacing

Progressive Overload & Lower ABV

Fuel Performance: Push for small load or rep progressions on your key lifts while keeping hydration high. On social nights, alternate 1-for-1 with sparkling water and shift to lower-ABV choices.

Week 4 (Days 22–30) Peak Consistency

Sustainable High-Clarity Lifestyle

Intentional Living: Set pre-decided caps before dinners or events, start with an NA drink in hand, and let your 30-day weight trend, lifting PRs, and mental clarity reinforce your new normal.

High-Impact Daily Health Habits

  • ✓ Protect the sleep window: Finish calories and alcohol 3+ hours before bed to maximize Garmin sleep score and next-morning mental clarity.
  • ✓ Upgrade evening rituals: Keep cold NA beers, hop waters, or sparkling water with bitters and lime front-and-center in the fridge.
  • ✓ Track the full feedback loop: Watch how dry nights directly improve Resting HR, workout consistency, and your 7-day weight trend.
